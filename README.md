# 💸 Dinero

Dinero is a Chrome extension that helps prevent impulse spending in real time.

Instead of tracking purchases after you spend, Dinero steps in at checkout with personalized reminders based on your goals and spending habits.

---

# ✨ What Dinero Does

- Helps you pause before impulse purchases
- Shows personalized reminders at checkout
- Encourages saving toward your goals
- Adds friction before spending decisions

Example:

> “You said you’re saving for a trip to Miami. Do you still want to buy these shoes right now?”

---

# 🚀 How to Install Dinero

## Step 1: Download Dinero

1. Click the green **Code** button near the top of this page
2. Click **Download ZIP**
3. Open the downloaded ZIP file
4. Extract/unzip the folder somewhere easy to find (like your Desktop)

---

## Step 2: Open Chrome Extensions

Open Google Chrome and go to:

```text
chrome://extensions
```

---

## Step 3: Turn On Developer Mode

In the top-right corner of the page, turn on:

```text
Developer mode
```

---

## Step 4: Load Dinero Into Chrome

1. Click:

```text
Load unpacked
```

2. Select the unzipped `dinero-extension` folder

Dinero should now appear in your Chrome extensions.

---

## Step 5: Start Using Dinero

1. Click the Dinero extension icon in Chrome
2. Fill out your preferences
3. Visit a shopping or checkout page
4. Dinero will step in with personalized reminders before purchases

---

# ⚠️ Current MVP Limitations

Dinero is currently an early MVP/prototype.

Some features are still in development:
- Price detection may not always be perfectly accurate
- Savings transfers are currently simulated
- Personalization is currently rules-based
